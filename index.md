    <h1>Taeyoung Ko</h1>
    <p>
      I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Design at UNIST, advised by Kyungho Lee. I believe that much of what gives meaning to our digital experiences is quiet, unnamed, and easy to miss. These qualities shape how we feel, what we choose, and what we remember, yet they often slip past the tools and methods we usually bring to digital content. I study these overlooked traces in everyday digital life, combining computational analysis with human-centered design to turn them into something useful. I call this Vibe Mining: surfacing the intangible qualities that shape how we experience and create, and turning them into materials for analysis and design. My work moves between two directions: understanding these qualities at scale through computational methods, and building human-AI systems that help people make sense of, navigate, and work with them.
    </p>
