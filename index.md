    <p>
      I am a Ph.D. candidate in the <a href="https://hcdt.unist.ac.kr/" target="_blank">Department of Design</a> at <a href="https://www.unist.ac.kr/" target="_blank">UNIST</a>, working in the <a href="https://klee141.com/" target="_blank">Expressive Computing Lab</a> with Prof. Kyungho Lee. My research places emphasis on multimodal communication, computational analysis of video, and human-centered design of creative tools.
    </p>
    <p>
      I study <span class="telop-word play"><span class="sr-only">telops</span><span aria-hidden="true"><span style="--i:0">t</span><span style="--i:1">e</span><span style="--i:2">l</span><span style="--i:3">o</span><span style="--i:4">p</span><span style="--i:5">s</span></span></span> (also known as impact captions), the stylized on-screen text widely used in online video. My approach treats on-screen text as a design medium that works with speech, sound, and images. Specifically, through it, (1) creators interact invisibly through vernacular design, borrowing each other’s styles until shared conventions form without formal guidelines, (2) creators and viewers interact through cues that direct attention and convey emotion, and (3) viewers share context through the same cues.
    </p>
    <p>
      Through large-scale computational analysis, I examine the roles and conventions of on-screen text across contexts. I am also interested in tools that let AI support creators’ expression rather than replace people.
    </p>
